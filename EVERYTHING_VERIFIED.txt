Correct Way to Reference Assets:

✅ Images
   <img src="/img/banner/slide-01.jpg" alt="Banner" />

   <i className="fas fa-home"></i>

❌ Wrong Ways (DON'T USE)
   <img src="./img/banner/slide-01.jpg" />
   <img src="../public/img/banner/slide-01.jpg" />
   <img src="adult-ai/img/banner/slide-01.jpg" />
